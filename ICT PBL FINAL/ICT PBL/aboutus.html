A Little About TEZDEN

TEZDEN is our group project focused on Disaster Risk Reduction, especially the impact of heatwaves on farming and agriculture in Central Myanmar. We explore how extreme heat affects crops, why farmers face these challenges, and what can be done to reduce the risks. By sharing what we learn, we hope to make useful information easier for others to understand.

Our Vision, Mission & Values

These three principles guide our research, decisions, and teamwork throughout the TEZDEN project.

Our Vision

To contribute to a future where farmers and communities in Central Myanmar are better prepared for heatwaves and can protect their crops more effectively.

Our Mission

To research heatwaves, understand their effects on agriculture, and share reliable information and practical ideas that can help reduce risks to farming.

Our Values

We value teamwork, responsibility, curiosity, reliable research, and respect for the experiences of farmers and the needs of our community.
