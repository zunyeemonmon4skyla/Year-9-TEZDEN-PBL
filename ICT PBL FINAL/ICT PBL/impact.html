Understand the impacts

What are the impacts of heatwaves?

Heatwaves can affect agriculture, the economy, human health, and the environment. They can cause crop failures, water shortages, financial losses, and other challenges for communities.

Impacts on Agriculture

How extreme heat affects crops, soil and livestock

Impacts on the Economy

How heatwaves affect income, prices, and business activities.

AGRICULTURE / 01

Crop failure and reduced yields

Extreme heat can damage crops, interfere with flowering and grain formation, and make plants lose water faster. These effects can reduce harvests and farmers' income.

AGRICULTURE / 02

Soil dryness and water shortages

High temperatures increase evaporation from soil and water sources. Soil can become too dry for crops, while farmers may struggle to find enough water for irrigation.

AGRICULTURE / 03

Livestock stress and deaths

During heatwaves, animals can suffer heat stress, eat less, and produce less milk or meat. Without shade and sufficient drinking water, severe heat can also cause livestock deaths.

ECONOMY / 01

Lower income for farmers

When heatwaves damage crops and reduce harvests, farmers have fewer products to sell. This can lower their income and make it harder to cover farming expenses and household needs.

ECONOMY / 02

Higher food prices

Heatwaves can reduce the supply of agricultural products. When fewer crops are available in markets, food prices may rise, making it more difficult for families to afford essential food.

ECONOMY / 03

Lower worker productivity

Extreme heat can make physical work difficult and reduce workers' productivity. Outdoor workers may need more breaks, causing delays and reducing the amount of work completed.

ECONOMY / 04

Higher operating costs

Businesses may spend more money on electricity, cooling systems, and water during heatwaves. These additional expenses can increase operating costs and reduce business profits.
